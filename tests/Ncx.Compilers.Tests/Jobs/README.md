# Jobs

The tests of the job compiler (`src/Ncx.Compilers/JobCompiler.cs`, implementation 16, P6-02): the NCX programs of the channels of a job in, one Fanuc program per channel out. `JobCompile.cs` compiles a job of NCX texts with the Fanuc compiler for a twin-turret lathe of G-code system A given as text, with the sub spindle, the wait marks `M100` to `M199`, `[spindle_sync]` bound as a test needs it and the door that only channel 1 may command.

Start with `JobCompilerTests.cs` (`SYNC` with the paths of the job, `start_mark`, the ranges of the `[sync]` groups, two programs of one file, the subprograms of a channel, the deadlock, also the one a generated `SYNC` makes with `START_CHANNEL`), then `JobBindingTests.cs` (a word bound to another channel moved to it at the same mark, a `channels = "all"` word duplicated behind a generated wait, also when an expansion rule or a rewriter (`DwellRewriter.cs`) generates the word, the `SKIP` of the block on the generated wait, the words of two blocks of one channel, or of two channels behind different marks, in one order in every program, and the ERRORs where the job compiler has no place or no one order for a word, or where its wait can never be released beside `WAIT_CHANNEL` or `START_CHANNEL`, D56), `ChannelBindingTests.cs` (the ERROR of a single-channel compile, virtual machine 3.8 rule 2a) and `ChannelOutputNamingTests.cs` (`O1000` and `O1000.P-2`, `_C1` and `_C2`, the program `NAME`).

The Nakamura pair compiled back as a job is in `../../Ncx.Acceptance/Jobs/NakamuraJobCompileTests.cs`, `ncx compile --job` in `../../Ncx.Acceptance/Cli/JobCommandTests.cs`.
