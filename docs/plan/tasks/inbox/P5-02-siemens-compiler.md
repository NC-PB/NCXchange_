  - Structure: the codes stand in `DiagnosticCodes.Siemens.cs` next to the folder, as the framework's `DiagnosticCodes.cs` asks of the families. `program_layout = "file_per_program"` (siemens 12 rule 1, one file per unit) is written as the archive of `one_file` and cut at its `%_N_` headers, `NAME.mpf` and `NAME.spf`, each subprogram once; `SiemensCompiler` re-implements `ICompiler.Compile` for it, so that the framework stays unchanged.
  - The main line is composed in the order `N G X Y Z F S T D M` of siemens 1; templates that are procedures (`COUPON(...)`, `CYCLE800(...)`, `CYCLE832(...)`), the tool change, `SETMS`, the frame instructions, `G74`/`G75`, `PRESETON`, `G4 F`, the calls, `WAITM`, `START`, `WAITE` stand in blocks of their own.   - Incremental words are `X=IC(5)`, never `G91`, so `G90` is written once where the control's group 14 is not known (the header, the first absolute motion of a subprogram); a `RAW` block makes every modal state unknown. The decimals of an address with an extension (`Z2`) are those of its letter (`Z`) where `[format] decimals` does not list it.
  - `SETMS(n)` is written in a block of its own where a template acts on the master spindle (a plain `S`, an M function, `SPOS=`, `LIMS=`, `G96`/`G97`) and another is selected; `SETMS` alone returns to the configured one, the spindle whose `CW` template is `M3`; a program starts with it, a subprogram from an unknown state (D99). `RPM` under `CSS` is not written (language 4.11: ignored while `CSS` is on), `CSS=OFF` writes `G97` and the `S` of `RPM`; `VC` outside `CSS` writes nothing.
  - The D of the control is `OFFSET`, or the one register of `OFFSET:LEN` and `OFFSET:RAD` (controller-mapping 3); the change template gets it as `{offset}`, and `D` is written in the main line where the change does not carry it; without `{offset}` the edge after a change is unknown.
  - The chain (D31): the first entry `TRANS`/`ROT RPL=`/`MIRROR`, the others `ATRANS`/`AROT RPL=`/`AMIRROR`; a removed entry of the programmable frame rewrites it from its first entry, `TRANS` alone where none stays (also for `ORIGIN`); a removed tilt is `TILT_OFF`. `{x} {y} {z}` of the tilt template are 0, since the chain carries the shifts, as the Fanuc compiler does. `ORIGIN` is written on change; a `[workpiece]` template that selects a settable frame counts as that datum.
  - Cycles: `MCALL` with the call for a `CYCLE_CALL` at a position, the position block with `G0` on change; `MCALL` alone before a motion that is no call, before a `CALL` and for `CYCLE=OFF`, only where the control has an `MCALL` active; a `CYCLE_CALL` without axis words is a direct call. `RTP` is `CLEARANCE` or `SAFE` per `CYCLE_RETRACT`, `SDIS` the distance of `CLEARANCE` from `SURFACE`, `_AXN` only where the drilling axis is not the tool axis, the fixed values at their positions, the trailing empty positions left out.
  - Flow: labels `NAME:` in a line of their own; an integer label, a name whose first two characters are no letters and a statement of siemens 8 and 9 (`LOOP`, `START`) get `LABEL_` in front; `JUMP` is `GOTOF` or `GOTOB` by where the label stands; `JUMP=END` and `RETURN` in a program go to the label `PROGRAM_END` before `M30`; a `CALL` under `IF` is `IF NOT (cond) GOTOF SKIP_CALL_<line>` over it, the structured forms never reconstructed (controller-mapping 6); a subprogram named by a number is `L100`; its `PROC` declares the `ARG` names of its calls as `REAL` in the order they first stand, the calls give them by position and the caller announces it with `EXTERN`; `CALL="name"` is `EXTCALL("name")`. In an expression the comparisons stand in parentheses under `AND`, `OR` and `NOT`, which bind stronger on the control; `^2` is `POT`, `INT` `TRUNC`, `ATAN(x)` `ATAN2(x,1)`, `FRAC(x)` `(x-TRUNC(x))`, a further power and `SGN` are CMP590.
  - A `SUB_frame = "datum"` side negates Z (machine-config 5) in the axis words, the centers, the shifts, the cycle planes along Z and the tool vector, and turns `G2`/`G3` and `G41`/`G42` in the planes that hold Z.
