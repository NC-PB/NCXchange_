# P6-01 Job scheduler and `SYNC`

Phase: 6 | Milestone: M9 | Depends on: `P4-01` | Size: M (S: a day or two, M: up to a week, L: more)

## Goal

Several channels in one run, advanced in rounds, with rendezvous marks (D39).

## Scope

- One VM per channel program from the job manifest; rounds where every channel that is neither finished nor waiting executes one block; `SYNC=m` with `WITH` (default all), marks matched in execution order, `WAIT_CHANNEL`, `START_CHANNEL`; deadlock ERROR naming the marks; shared resources from `[shared]` with the WARNING for two channels on one spindle between marks.
- `ncx analyze --job <ncxjob.toml>` and `ncx check --job`.

## References

- ncx-virtual-machine.md section 3.7
- architecture.md section 5.4 (scheduler flowchart)
- machine-config.md section 8

## Done when

- A two-channel test job with three marks in different orders passes; a job whose channels wait at different marks reports the deadlock with both marks.
- The Nakamura WY250L pair reads (phase 3 reader plus the builder tables) and analyzes without deadlock.

- The virtual machine runs step by step (architecture 5, `Step`): the walks of both modes (`RunSection`, `FollowInterpretedCall`, `EnterInterpretedSub`, `CallExternalProgram` of INTERPRETED mode, `WalkSection`, `FollowCall`, `EnterSub` of STATIC mode) hand out one step per executed block, also inside a called subprogram or external program, as C# iterators, and set how the section ended in a `SectionEnd`; `Begin` starts one program on its channel, `Step` executes the next block, `Abandon` gives a run up. `RunInterpreted` and `Run` take the steps one after the other, and every earlier test passes unchanged. A block the flow passes without executing it (a skipped `SKIP` block, `IF` 0) takes no round. A code-structure choice: a hand-written frame stack would have rewritten the flow of P4-01.
- Rounds (VM 3.7, architecture 5.4): every channel neither finished nor waiting executes one block, in the order of the manifest; after the round `WAIT_CHANNEL` is released once the awaited channel has finished, and `SYNC=m` once every participant waits at m, all participants together. `SYNC_WAIT` is raised in the round a channel reaches its mark, `SYNC_RELEASE` for every participant in the round of the release, both through the listeners of the channel with `channel.waitingAt` changing between their `Before` and `After` (VM 2.8, 7); `JobResult.Timeline` keeps them. The channel of a `SYNC` always takes part, also when `WITH` leaves it out ("the channel waits until every participating channel has reached" the mark, language 4.8). `SYNC` in a job of one channel does not wait, and the pre-pass warns (`VM570`, now counting the channels of the job; a file alone still decides by its programs, D196).
- An ERROR of a channel stops the whole job once the round is over (VM 2.9); the channels are given up where they stand, and a channel inside an external program hands the diagnostics of the call back to its file (a `finally` in `CallExternalProgram`). The deadlock (`VM571`) is one ERROR on the block the first waiting channel waits at, naming for every channel its mark with its participants, its `WAIT_CHANNEL`, the `START_CHANNEL` it waits for, or that it has finished.
- `[shared]` (`SharedResources`, D20): a spindle or axis of `[shared]` commanded by two channels that have not synchronized since (a mark both waited at, a `WAIT_CHANNEL` of one for the other, a `START_CHANNEL` of one by the other) is the WARNING, once per pair of channels and resource between marks, on the later block naming the earlier (`VM572` for spindles, `VM850` for axes). A block commands a spindle with `SPINDLE`, `RPM`, `CSS`, `VC`, `RPM_MAX`, `ORIENT`, `SPINDLE_MODE` and both spindles of `SPINDLE_SYNC`, as step 2 resolved them; it commands an axis with an axis word; `[shared] axes` names axes by id and is mapped to their NCX names through `[[axis]]`.
