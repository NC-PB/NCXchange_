# Controller Mapping

Status: Version 1.0 of the specification set, 2026-09-12 (draft 8 in the drafting history of `../decisions/decisions.md`). How readers derive NCX words from Fanuc, Heidenhain and Siemens source, and what compilers write. "TOML" means the value comes from the machine configuration. The Fanuc and Heidenhain columns come from the controller manuals and the four example programs; the Siemens column was read against the SINUMERIK 840D sl programming manual (NC programming 06/2019, V4.92, with the externally programmable cycles in chapter 3.25) and the older 840D fundamentals, job planning and cycles manuals. Section 8 collects what the machine-builder manuals add on top of the controller manuals and what the Siemens machines of the sample corpus add on top of that; section 10 lists the sources by title; section 11 is the survey of the sample corpus.

Readers resolve modal G0/G1/G2/G3 and G90/G91 with the source-side VM so that every NCX motion block carries its verb and absolute or incremental words as written. A Fanuc `G91` block becomes `IX`, `IY` words; the compiler for Heidenhain writes `IX`, the compiler for Fanuc may write `G91` blocks or absolute values, per TOML.

Reader rules that need the source-side VM: Fanuc `T4 M6` when `T4` was preloaded earlier gives `TOOL=4` and clears the preload; `M6` without any preload is an ERROR in the source; `T5` after an `M6` in the same block (`T4 M6 T5` is illegal on most controls, the reader reports it). Heidenhain `TOOL CALL` with an `S` sets `RPM`; `TOOL CALL S2000` without number (allowed on newer TNCs) sets only `RPM`. The two-step ATC forms of the builders fit the two-word model without special cases: Mori Seiki `T9001` is the `PRELOAD` and `G361` the `TOOL` with the preloaded number (D91); Nakamura `G340 T.. A..` is `TOOL` plus `PRELOAD` in one block; DMG `T="NAME"` plus `TC(...)` is one `TOOL` (the `TC` call is the change, the `T` word alone does nothing on the DMG). A Fanuc lathe `T` code in a block of its own moves the axes by the offset difference (rapid under G0, feed otherwise): the reader emits `OFFSET` and the VM does not move; the compiler writes the `T` code into a `G0` block as the builders recommend.

Heidenhain readers decide between `SUB` and `LABEL` by usage: a `LBL n` that is called without `REP` and closed by `LBL 0` is a `SUB` (written as a `SUB=BEGIN NAME=n` section of the file); a `LBL n` used by `REP` or as a `GOTO` target is a `LABEL`. Siemens readers take every `%_N_name_SPF` unit and every `PROC` unit of a file as a `SUB` section and every `%_N_name_MPF` unit as a `PROGRAM`; a `%_N_name_INI` unit (tool data, `[MAFU]` machine function groups in the INDEX archives) is not NC code and is kept as a `RAW:SIEMENS` block list of its own program section, so that nothing is lost (D5).

Fanuc `M99` in the main program (no subprogram active) does not end the program: it jumps to the first block, which is how bar-work programs loop (Nakamura and Biglia manuals). The reader writes `LABEL=START` after the header and turns that `M99` into `JUMP=START`; the block-skip form `/M99` becomes `SKIP JUMP=START`. A block-skipped `/M30` or `/M02` is likewise not the program end (it is still executed as an end when the switch is off): the NCX reader writes `SKIP JUMP=END` there and the real `PROGRAM=END` at the end of the program. `M99 Pn` in a subprogram returns to block `n` of the caller: `RETURN` plus `JUMP` in NCX, kept as the two words.

## 9. Things readers keep as RAW

`PLANE` forms other than `SPATIAL` and `AXIAL`, `FN 16`/`FN 18`/`FN 19`, unmapped system variables, macro `G66` modal calls, `G10` data setting (datum and offset writes), probing and measuring cycles, `G38` push checks, builder macros such as `G411`, `G300`, `G131`, `G359`, `L730`, `L770`, `CHECK_MAG`, `SETPIECE` until the catalog or a `FUNC` covers them. Siemens: `MSG`, `STOPRE`, `$P_UIFR` and other data writes, `WORKPIECE(...)` blank definitions (header information like `BLK FORM`), `DEF` declarations of non-numeric types, synchronized actions (`ID=`, `WHEN`, `DO`), `SETINT`/`LIFTFAST`, `ORIWKS`/`ORIMKS`/`ORIAXES`/`ORIVECT`, `NORM`/`KONT`/`G450`/`G451`/`CFC`/`CFTCP`, `G60`/`G601`/`G64`/`G641`..`G645` with `ADIS`, `COMPCAD`, `FGROUP`/`FGREF`/`FL`, `SBLOF`/`DISPLOF`, `GROUP_BEGIN`/`GROUP_END` (read as `SECTION`), the `<PROG_BEGIN_C1>` recipe markers of the STAMA post, and every `%_N_name_INI` unit. The NT NURSE branch commands `G471`..`G486` and `G480` are not `RAW`: they are `JUMP` with `IF`. Each `RAW` is a WARNING at read time and an ERROR at compile time for another controller or builder. A `CYCLE:<controller>=n` block with its native parameters gets the same ERROR at compile time for another controller family, without the read-time WARNING (D94).
