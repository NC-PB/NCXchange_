# Fanuc

The reader of Fanuc and ISO programs (controllers `fanuc.md` 1 to 9; the Fanuc column of every section of `controller-mapping.md`): one file per concern, so that an NC programmer finds `G81` in `FanucCycles.cs` and `T5` in `FanucToolWords.cs`.

Start with `FanucReader.cs`. It gives the structure pass the role of every block (`%`, `O`, `N`, `GOTO`, `M30`, `M2`, `M99`, the calls of `M98 P` and `G65 P`), writes the complete header of D34 after `PROGRAM=BEGIN` (`FanucHeader.cs`), and reads every block concern by concern into a `FanucDraft` of NCX blocks: `FanucMacro.cs` (`#n` to `Vn`, `[ ]` to `{ }` through `FanucExpression.cs`, `IF GOTO`, `IF THEN`, `WHILE DO END`, `GOTO`, `JUMP=END`, the NT NURSE branch commands `G480`, `G471`..`G476`, `G481`..`G486` of the builder nakamura, `G65`, `M98`, the `M200` of the builder nakamura, `M99`), `FanucFrames.cs` (`G54`.., `G52`, `G53`, `G28`/`G30`, `G92`/`G50`, `G68`, `G51.1`, `G43.4`/`G43.5`, `G12.1`, `G7.1`, `G5.1`) with `FanucTilt.cs` (`G68.2`, `G53.1`), `FanucToolWords.cs` (`T`, `M6`, the turret `T0656`, the builder's ATC through `[tool_change]`, `G43 H`, `G49`, `D`, the `S` binding rule, `G96`/`G97`), `FanucCycles.cs` (`G73`..`G89`, `G98`/`G99`, `K`, `G80`, lathe drilling with `AXIS`, the turning cycles of the catalog, `G70`..`G76` with `CONTOUR`, whose contour the structure pass moves into a `SUB` section), `FanucMotion.cs` (`G0`..`G3`, `G90`/`G91` and `U W H V`, `I J K` and `R`, `F`, `G17`..`G19`, `G20`/`G21`, `G94`/`G95`, `G40`..`G42`, `G4`) with `FanucCorners.cs` (`,C` and `,R`, D58) and `FanucPolarCoordinates.cs` (`G16`), and `FanucBuilder.cs` (the M codes through the machine's tables, `MFUNC`, `[workpiece]`, the wait marks of `[sync]`, and the codes kept as `RAW`). Every word a concern reads is marked in `FanucBlock`; a word that no concern reads keeps its block as `RAW` with a WARNING, so nothing is dropped (D5).

`FanucTokenizer.cs` cuts the file into blocks, `FanucModalGroups.cs` gives the modal groups of the machine's G-code system (A, B, C, or none on a mill), `FanucAxes.cs` says which NCX axis an address moves, `FanucState.cs` holds what the reader knows of the file beyond the modal groups: `FanucChain.cs` the chain of transforms it wrote (language 4.2), and `FanucCalls.cs` with `FanucCallerState.cs`, `FanucChanges.cs` and `FanucUnknowns.cs` the state a subprogram runs with and the state it leaves its caller (virtual machine 3.9), with what the reader does not know of them. The codes are `RDR100` to `RDR299` in `../DiagnosticCodes.Fanuc.cs`.

Never here: `ncx convert` (`Ncx.Cli`), the meaning of the program beyond what the source leaves implicit (the virtual machine), code for one machine (its TOML file: the builder's codes come through `[tool_change]`, `[transform]`, `[workpiece]`, `[sync]` and `[raw]`).
