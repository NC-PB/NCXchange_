# Siemens

The compiler of SINUMERIK 840D and 840D sl programs (controllers `siemens.md` 12; the Siemens column of every section of `controller-mapping.md`; machine-config 2, 3, 5): one file per concern, so that an NC programmer finds `CYCLE800` in `SiemensFrames.cs` and `MCALL` in `SiemensCycles.cs`.

Start with `SiemensCompiler.cs`. It writes every block concern by concern into a `SiemensBlock` (the block with its state before and after, the main line `SiemensLine` in the order `N G X Y Z F S T D M` of siemens 1, and the words written, so that a word no concern writes is the ERROR `CMP500` and never dropped), and under `program_layout = "file_per_program"` cuts the archive into one file per unit (`SiemensUnits.cs`). The concerns: `SiemensProgramFrame.cs` (rule 1: `%_N_NAME_MPF` and `%_N_NAME_SPF`, `PROC` with the parameters of the calls, `EXTERN`, `program_end` behind the label a `JUMP=END` goes to, `sub_end`, the comments), `SiemensMotion.cs` with `SiemensAxes.cs` and `SiemensArcs.cs` (rule 2: `G0` to `G3`, the equals sign after an address with an extension, `IC()`, `DC()`, `I=AC()`, `CR=`, `AR=`, `TURN=` or the turns of an older 840D, `G94`/`G95`, `G40` to `G42`, `A3= B3= C3=` under `TRAORI`, `G53`, `G4 F`, the diameter of X and the Z of a sub spindle side with a datum), `SiemensTools.cs` and `SiemensSpindles.cs` (rule 3: `T`, `M6` and `D` per `[tool_change]`, `SETMS(n)` in a block of its own, `S2=` and `M2=3`, `G96`, `LIMS`, `SPOS`, `COUPON`/`COUPOF`), `SiemensFunctions.cs` (the M functions of `[coolant]` and `[func]`, `MFUNC`, `M0`/`M1`, `[workpiece]`), `SiemensFrames.cs` (rule 4: `G54`.., the chain as `TRANS` and `ATRANS`/`AROT`/`AMIRROR`, `CYCLE800` from `[transform]` with `{dir}` from `MOVE`, `G74`/`G75`, `PRESETON`, `DIAMON`/`DIAMOF`, `TRAORI`, `TRACYL`, `TRANSMIT`, `CYCLE832`, and `TRANS` alone or `CYCLE800()` where a block ends a programmable frame or a swivel that a `RAW` line may have made), `SiemensCycles.cs` (rule 5: the signature of the catalog entry, `MCALL`, the direct call, the modal `F` of `CYCLE_F`, `_AXN`), `SiemensFlow.cs` with `SiemensExpressions.cs` (`IF ... GOTOF`, labels `NAME:`, `L100`, `NAME P3`, `EXTCALL`, `REPEAT`, `R1=`, the expressions), `SiemensChannels.cs` (rule 6: `WAITM` from `[sync]`, `INIT`, `START`, `WAITE`); `RAW:SIEMENS` is written verbatim by `SiemensCompiler.cs`, the `N` number of its source block included, which `block_numbers` gives no other line. `SiemensFile.cs` holds what a block needs of the whole file: the unit names, the parameters of the subprograms, the labels, and what the control has active at the jump over a conditional call. `SiemensBlock.cs` keeps an unknown value of the target state (after `RAW`, after a label) as a value of its own, so that it survives the return of a subprogram.

The codes are `CMP500` to `CMP699` in `../DiagnosticCodes.Siemens.cs`. The tests are in `../../../tests/Ncx.Compilers.Tests/Siemens/`, the acceptance with `MILLTURN_TRANSFER.ncx` and the round trip through the reader in `../../../tests/Ncx.Acceptance/Examples/SiemensCompilerTests.cs`.

Never here: the meaning of a word (the virtual machine's), code for one machine (its TOML file and the cycle catalog `cycles/siemens.toml`), the GILDEMEISTER structure programming (D68: documented in `docs/controllers/machine-builders.md`, not implemented).
